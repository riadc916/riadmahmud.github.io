About

Riad Mahmud Chowdhury is currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science degree in Computer Science and Technology at the School of Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science, Nantong University, Jiangsu Province, People’s Republic of China (expected graduation 2027), maintaining a perfect CGPA of 4.00/4.00. His research and technical interests span Data Science, Machine Learning (ML), and Cybersecurity across the end-to-end data lifecycle, from data preprocessing and exploratory data analysis (EDA) to online learning and model deployment. His recent research focuses on game-theoretic moving-target defense and cyber-physical network security, where he has worked on simulation, security analysis, and online learning–based defense evaluation on IEEE 14-bus and 118-bus benchmark power grids. Building on this work, he aims to develop robust, scalable defense algorithms while investigating methods to make machine learning workflows more energy-efficient and environmentally sustainable.

[Time-Series EDA] StackOverflow Language Trends — Developer Adoption Analytics · 2026 [CODE]

Applied pivot tables and 12-month rolling-mean smoothing, revealing JavaScript as the cumulative all-time leader (2,056,510 posts) and Python’s rise to #1 monthly active language (~34,478 posts/month by 2020).
[Python & Pandas] College Salaries — Post-Graduation Compensation Modeling · 2026 [CODE]

Identified Physician Assistant as the top starting major ($74,300), Chemical Engineering as the highest mid-career median earner ($107,000), Nursing as the lowest-risk career ($50,700 10th–90th percentile spread) vs. Economics with the highest upside ($210,000 90th percentile), and showed STEM majors lead in group-level mid-career median pay ($90,812.50).
[R & Tidyverse] Superstore Sales & Profitability — Enterprise Retail Analytics · 2026 [CODE]
